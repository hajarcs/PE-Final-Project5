# IT-Rules-v1_2026_10_15

Responsible-use rules for the Internal IT Help Desk.
Each rule says who, what, and when, and can be answered with yes or no.

**Owner:** Hajar Alanazi · **Version:** v1 · **Date:** 2026-10-15

| # | Area | Rule | Yes / no test |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Data | Before using a support ticket in an AI prompt, the agent replaces all employee names, IDs, phone numbers, passwords and verification codes with masked values such as User-1 or Request-X. | Were all personal and sensitive details removed or masked before testing? |
| 2 | Verification | Before sending an AI-generated reply, the technician checks it against the five output-quality criteria and sends it only if it scores 5 out of 5. | Did the reply pass all five checks before sending? |
| 3 | Fairness | Before a template enters the library, its owner tests it with two different employee requests and one edge case, and records the results. | Were two normal inputs and one edge case tested and recorded? |
| 4 | Policy | The agent uses only the approved IT-Facts-v1 information and does not invent support procedures, promises or actions. | Is every support instruction supported by the approved IT facts? |
| 5 | Human decision | AI may draft, summarize, classify, simplify or translate support information, but a human technician reviews the output before it is sent or acted upon. | Did a human technician review the output before use? |
